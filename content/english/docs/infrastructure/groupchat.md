## Why Signal?
- Send photos (resizes to manageable size, can add filters) 
- Quick, informal communication (in the team and with partners) 
- Doesn't clog email   
- Group chat
- Use Signal on your computer, much quicker typing! [signal.org/download](https://signal.org/download/) 
## How to setup Signal
1. Add contacts to `Your Contacts` in Outlook
2. [Install Signal](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/signal-private-messenger/id874139669) on iPhone 
3. Add Microsoft Exchange account on iPhone
### 1. Add contacts
For any person you want to Signal, you have to **add them and their mobile phone number to Your Contacts** in Outlook
### 2. Install Signal on iPhone
![](https://res.cloudinary.com/naturceredigion/image/upload/w_320/v1795513984/261124-signal-apple-store.jpg)
### 3. Add Microsoft Exchange on iPhone
We want to add a Microsoft Exchange account to your iPhone Contact accounts
![](https://res.cloudinary.com/naturceredigion/image/upload/v1795512615/10-iphone.webp)
1. Open Settings 
2. Open Contact Settings 
3. Add Accounts and select Microsoft Exchange 
4. Add your email  
5. Click Continue to allow microsoftonline.com 
6. Select Contacts to synchronise them
