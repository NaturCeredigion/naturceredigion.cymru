### Ceredigion Tree Warden Network
Become a Tree Warden with the Ceredigion Local Nature Partnership.

Do you have a few hours to spare and want to make a real difference for nature in your community?

We're looking for volunteers to join our Tree Warden team. No experience is needed – we'll provide:
- Full training
- PPE (Personal Protective Equipment)
- All the equipment you’ll need will be provided, within reason.

At the moment, our greatest need is for volunteers to help water newly planted trees in Talybont and Aberystwyth, but we're keen to recruit Tree Wardens from every part of Ceredigion to support future planting and tree care across the county.

Get in touch with [TreeWardenNetwork@ceredigion.gov.uk](mailto:TreeWardenNetwork@ceredigion.gov.uk "mailto:TreeWardenNetwork@ceredigion.gov.uk") today to find out more and become part of a growing network of people caring for our local environment.

![Student hand on mossy Sycamore tree](https://res.cloudinary.com/naturceredigion/image/upload/v1778765442/260508-aberaeron-workshop3-student-tree-moss.jpg)

Together, we can help Ceredigion's trees flourish for generations to come.
